Connect your visitor database

This page shows who visits the Spice N Cook site: approximate city, device, where they came from, how long they stayed and what they did. It needs a free Supabase database to store visits.

  1. Create a free project at supabase.com.
  2. In the project, open SQL Editor, paste supabase/insights.sql from the repo, change the passphrase at the bottom, and press Run.
  3. From Project Settings → API, copy the Project URL and the anon / publishable key into insights in js/menu.js, then push.
  4. Come back here and sign in with your passphrase.
